NATIONAL CYBER CRIME REPORTING PORTAL (PROTOTYPE)
EVIDENCE CHECKLIST
=====================================================

Always collect:
  [ ] Screenshots of chats, emails, SMS alerts and call logs
  [ ] Screenshot of the fraudulent payment / checkout page
  [ ] Bank statement pages showing the debits (with UTR numbers)
  [ ] UPI ID, account number or wallet ID of the beneficiary
  [ ] Phone numbers, email headers, website URLs and handles used
  [ ] A short timeline (date + time + what happened)

Category-specific additions
  UPI / payment fraud ....... transaction IDs, QR code image,
                               app payment history export
  OTP / banking fraud ....... SMS alerts, SIM replacement SMS,
                               bank complaint reference number
  Social media .............. profile URLs, post/comment links,
                               screenshots showing timestamps
  Sextortion / harassment ... do NOT pay; preserve chats, note
                               the first contact time, report fast
  Job / investment fraud .... group links, deposit screenshots,
                               fake app name and version
  Ransomware / hacking ...... ransom note, file extensions,
                               system logs, backup status

Tips
  - File names should be clear (e.g. upi-debit-12nov.png).
  - Do not edit or crop screenshots — full screens help officers.
  - Do not include your passwords or OTPs in any file.
