NATIONAL CYBER CRIME REPORTING PORTAL (PROTOTYPE)
CYBER SAFETY CHECKLIST
=====================================================

ACCOUNTS
  [ ] Unique password for every important account
  [ ] Two-factor authentication enabled (app-based where possible)
  [ ] Review active sessions monthly and sign out unknown devices
  [ ] Never reuse your email password on other sites

PAYMENTS
  [ ] Never scan a QR code to RECEIVE money
  [ ] Verify payee name before approving any UPI collect request
  [ ] Keep card limits and international usage toggled off by default
  [ ] Turn on transaction alerts for every debit

DEVICES
  [ ] Screen lock with PIN/biometric on phone and laptop
  [ ] Install apps only from official app stores
  [ ] Update OS and apps automatically
  [ ] Never install remote-access apps on a caller's request

COMMUNICATION
  [ ] Treat urgency as a red flag
  [ ] Verify lookalike domains before clicking
  [ ] No bank, wallet or official will ask for OTP / PIN / CVV
  [ ] When unsure, call the number on the back of your card

IF FRAUD HAPPENS
  1. Call 1930 immediately
  2. Ask your bank to freeze the beneficiary account
  3. File a complaint on the portal with evidence
  4. Track the complaint and respond to officer queries
