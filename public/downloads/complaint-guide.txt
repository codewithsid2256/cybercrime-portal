NATIONAL CYBER CRIME REPORTING PORTAL (PROTOTYPE)
COMPLAINT REGISTRATION GUIDE
=====================================================

DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT — This prototype is not an official
Government of India service. For real complaints use
cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.

STEP 1 — CHOOSE A CATEGORY
  Select the crime type that best fits what happened. If you are
  unsure, choose "Other Cyber Crime" and describe it — an officer
  will re-classify the complaint.

STEP 2 — YOUR DETAILS
  Provide your full name, a working mobile number, email address
  and your state/district. Your name is never shown publicly.

STEP 3 — INCIDENT DETAILS
  - Date and approximate time of the incident.
  - Amount lost (approximate is fine).
  - A clear description: how you were contacted, what was sent,
    what you did, and what the loss was.
  - Any suspect identifiers: phone numbers, UPI IDs, bank
    accounts, URLs or social handles.

STEP 4 — EVIDENCE AND SUBMISSION
  Attach up to five files (max 5 MB each): screenshots, bank
  statements, PDFs, or a short video. Accept the declaration,
  answer the security question and submit.

AFTER SUBMISSION
  - You receive an NCRP complaint number instantly.
  - Track status any time with the complaint number and the
    registered mobile number.
  - For money already transferred, call 1930 first — the first
    hour matters most.
